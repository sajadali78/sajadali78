## 🧭 About me

I am an electrical machines researcher based in Sukkur, Pakistan.
I design, model and simulate advanced electrical machines.
My main focus is dual-stator hybrid-rotor machines.
I work with finite element analysis in Ansys Maxwell and PyAEDT.
I also build machine-learning surrogate models to speed up design.
I teach as Visiting Faculty at Sukkur IBA University.
I am currently looking for a PhD position in electrical machines, in Europe or Asia.

---

## ⚙️ What I do

- **Machine design.** I design advanced electrical machines, with a focus on dual-stator hybrid-rotor topologies.
- **FEA modelling.** I model and simulate machines in Ansys Maxwell, and automate the work with PyAEDT.
- **Surrogate modelling.** I use physics-informed neural networks and Gaussian processes as fast surrogates for FEA.
- **Teaching tools.** I build web apps that help students learn machine geometry and present research data.

## 🔬 Research

**Area:** design, modelling and simulation of advanced electrical machines.

**Focus:** dual-stator hybrid-rotor machines, including machines that combine synchronous and vernier operation.

**Methods:**
- Finite element analysis in Ansys Maxwell
- Design automation with PyAEDT
- Machine-learning surrogate models: physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) and Gaussian processes
